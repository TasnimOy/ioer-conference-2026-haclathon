### Upcoming Contributions

*   **[Topic: Digital Landscape Traces]** - *Alexander Dunkel (IOER) & Dominik Weckmüller (TU Dresden)*  
    To support transformative governance and planning, this interactive chapter will explore a privacy-safe dataset of 66 million social media posts ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.71830/VDMUWW)) published in the [ioerDATA](https://data.fdz.ioer.de/)  to map patterns of visitor frequentation for selected regions and areas in Germany. Responding to direct feedback from planning offices and stakeholders, the notebook will provide an accessible approach to filter, subset, and visualize digital attention in physical spaces. The goal is to help planners identify spatial pressures and inform sustainable regional development. 
*   **[Topic: Settlement Monitoring]** - *Oliver Harig*\
    IBTool is an open-source QGIS plugin that automatically derives settlement boundaries from building footprints and street networks ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijgi10050353)). It replaces manually digitised or administratively defined delineations with a consistent, data-driven delineation of the inner zone (§ 34 BauGB). Practical applications include settlement-area monitoring, assessing infill-development potential, and documenting land consumption — scalable from the municipal to the nationwide level. A recent expert evaluation ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/23998083241308407)) found IBTool's delineations more precise and consistent than comparable products such as ATKIS®-Ortslage and GHSL, with notably less over-detection at settlement edges. To support practical use, I will contribute tutorials covering the application of the tool as well as guidance on data preparation.
